    What could go wrong?

    Straight answers to the hard questions

    Why not just use Shopify or Fygaro Shops?

    They are good stores, but neither ties the store to a WhatsApp conversation with order codes, automatic payment confirmation in the chat, and marketing built from the same catalog. ZDM ChatCart is built around how Caribbean customers already buy: in WhatsApp. A client already running a Fygaro store can keep it.

    What exactly does the setup fee pay for?

    Doing the hard part for the client: the payment account application and connection, WhatsApp Business number and Meta verification, Instagram and Facebook connection, domain, products and shop page, and a live test payment before launch. $1,500 one time, or $2,500 for All-in-One plans, which also need the ad account, Pixel and marketing set up.

    Does money pass through ZDM ChatCart?

    No. Each business has its own Fygaro account, so customer payments go straight to them. ZDM ChatCart only receives Fygaro's signed confirmation that an order was paid. ZDM ChatCart's own revenue is the monthly plan and setup fee.

    What does each client need from Fygaro and Meta?

    A Fygaro plan that allows fixed-amount payment links and payment notifications (Pro or above), a WhatsApp Business number connected through Meta, and an Instagram Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Meta verification and app review can take weeks, so they start on day one of each client.

    Are there hidden running costs for clients?

    WhatsApp charges per message for business-initiated messages, such as reminders outside the 24-hour reply window and promotional templates. Ad spend is set by the owner with a weekly cap. Both are paid by the client directly and shown up front.

    What if WhatsApp launches in-chat payments in the Caribbean?

    ZDM ChatCart is the catalog, order, CRM and marketing layer. Payment is one connector inside it, so a new payment method can plug in beside Fygaro without rebuilding.

    Can customers tap links on Instagram posts?

    Not in captions or comments: Instagram shows those as plain text. Links work in the bio, Story link stickers, ads and DMs. That is why ZDM ChatCart uses comment-to-DM: someone comments a keyword, the shop replies publicly and sends the link by DM, using Instagram’s official private replies. Each keyword’s link counts clicks and sales.

    Does it work for service businesses?

    Partly today. Fixed-price services, packages and deposits (a full set of nails, a deep clean, a photo session deposit) work like products: order on WhatsApp, pay by link, get a receipt. Appointments, staff calendars, time slots and reminders are the next build, as a Bookings option.

    How is customer data protected?

    Each business's orders, customers and keys are separated at the database level, so one client can never see another's data. Payment and messaging keys are stored server-side only and are never sent to the browser.

    What does the pilot prove?

    Over 30 days with one business that already takes WhatsApp orders: orders per week, time from first message to payment, how many payment links go unpaid, and hours of admin saved. That becomes the case study for the next five clients.
